### Project Overview

UPIQ is a personal finance management application designed to make expense tracking more convenient and accessible. It enables users to work with bank statement PDFs and review their financial activity through a centralized dashboard.

The application focuses on transforming transaction data into a more understandable financial overview, helping users examine expenses, identify spending patterns, and monitor their money without manually recording every transaction.

### Motivation & Problem

Tracking personal finances manually can be tedious, particularly when transactions are spread across monthly bank statements. Conventional workflows often require users to enter expenses individually or connect sensitive banking credentials to external financial aggregation services.

UPIQ explores a statement-based alternative: users provide their bank statement PDFs and use the application to review the resulting financial data.

The frontend experience centers on two challenges:

1. **Making Financial Data Understandable:** Raw transaction records are difficult to interpret at a glance. A structured dashboard helps users review expenses and identify spending patterns more easily.
2. **Simplifying Expense Tracking:** Users need a convenient way to navigate transaction records and financial summaries without relying on manual bookkeeping.
