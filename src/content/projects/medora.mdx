### Project Overview

Medora is a full-stack healthcare appointment booking platform that connects patients and doctors through a centralized scheduling workflow. It enables patients to discover doctors, review availability, book appointments, and track their appointment history, while doctors manage availability and handle incoming appointment requests through a dedicated dashboard.

The platform supports appointment statuses including `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `RESCHEDULED`, `REJECTED`, and `COMPLETED`, providing a structured lifecycle from booking request to completion.

### Motivation & Problem

Traditional clinic scheduling can involve unnecessary coordination between patients and medical staff. Patients may need to contact clinics to check doctor availability, while doctors need a consistent way to manage appointment requests and schedules.

Medora addresses these challenges through two core capabilities:

1. **Simplified Appointment Booking:** Patients can discover doctors, review available slots, and submit booking requests through a streamlined online workflow.
2. **Centralized Appointment Management:** Dedicated patient and doctor dashboards bring booking history, availability management, and appointment status updates into one platform.

The result is a more organized scheduling process with less manual coordination and transaction-protected booking operations.

### Key Engineering Decisions & Trade-Offs

#### 1. Database Transactions for Appointment Booking

- **Decision:** Used database transactions around booking operations to maintain consistency when appointments are requested.
- **Rationale:** Appointment scheduling requires reliable handling of availability and booking state. Transactional operations help prevent conflicting reservations and ensure related database changes are handled together.
- **Outcome:** Booking logic is structured around database consistency rather than relying solely on frontend availability checks.
